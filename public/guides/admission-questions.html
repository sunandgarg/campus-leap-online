Ten questions before you enrol

Ask the university directly and keep a written record of its answers.

  1. Where can I verify this exact programme, Online mode and academic session?
  2. Am I eligible, and which documents and identifiers are required?
  3. What is the complete current curriculum and specialisation availability?
  4. When are live classes, and is attendance compulsory?
  5. Can I replay recordings, and for how long?
  6. How are assignments, practical work and examinations conducted?
  7. What is the total payable fee, including every additional charge?
  8. What are the cancellation, refund, pause and withdrawal rules?
  9. Who handles learner support, and what response times can I expect?
  10. What career support is actually included, and what are its written terms?

Career support is not a job or salary guarantee. Apply and pay through the university’s official channels, and obtain a university receipt.
