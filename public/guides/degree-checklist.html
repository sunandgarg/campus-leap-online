Is this online degree right for us?

A checklist to discuss with your family. Keep the university’s written answers with your shortlist.

  1. Recognition: match the university’s legal name, exact programme, Online mode and academic session on the official UGC-DEB portal.
  2. Eligibility: confirm entry requirements, required documents and any professional-body requirements.
  3. Learning: ask for a platform demonstration, class timetable and recording-access terms.
  4. Assessment: confirm exam mode, proctoring requirements, attendance and assignment deadlines.
  5. Budget: write down the complete fee, additional charges, payment schedule and refund policy.
  6. Support: ask who handles academic, technical and administrative questions, and when they respond.
  7. Your goal: compare the curriculum with the skills you want. Check employer or further-study requirements where relevant.

Institutional recognition does not automatically confirm every programme or intake. Check current official records before paying.
